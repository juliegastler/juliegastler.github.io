Advanced Motion Diagram Car Lab

Build a sequence of motions, press Go, and watch the car's position get marked once each second.

Each leg lasts 6 seconds. Add as many legs as you like, including Stopped. During a stop, dots stack vertically at the same position, randomly upward or downward, and the next motion continues from the end of the stack. Check “Keep motion diagrams” to stack previous runs below the newest one; Reset clears all diagrams. Dots represent equal time intervals, not equal distances. Time labels can be shown or hidden. Sound begins when you press Go; your browser's audio settings may affect volume.
